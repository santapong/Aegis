# Cash-flow product references

Researched October 1, 2026, Asia/Bangkok. These are vendor product descriptions and documentation, not independent performance tests. Public Float and Monarch pages were inspected in the visible browser. Other references were read through the web tool. No competitor account was created, signed into, or tested. Proposed Aegis adaptations below are our inferences.

| Primary source | Observed pattern | Why it helps here | Proposed Aegis adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Actual Budget — Balance Forecast Report](https://actualbudget.org/docs/experimental/balance-forecast-report/) | Starts from posted records and expands schedules; exposes account/filter scope and daily or monthly granularity. The documentation labels this report experimental. | A forecast needs explicit inputs and dates, rather than unexplained averages. | Account-scoped dated cash, visible opening provenance, and an inspectable daily table. Do not infer that an experimental reference proves production readiness. |
| [Monarch — Tracking](https://www.monarch.com/features/tracking) | Recurring bills/subscriptions can be viewed as a calendar or list; transactions have a review workflow. The previous recurring URL redirects here. | Everyday users recognize bills and dates before analytical terminology. | Personal commitments agenda, review inbox, and short plain-language answers. No assumption of comparable bank connections in Aegis. |
| [PocketSmith — Calendar and Forecast](https://learn.pocketsmith.com/article/1246-using-the-calendar-and-forecast-graph) | Calendar budget events, selected scenarios, and projected account balances are connected. | Editing a date should change the graph and calendar consistently. | One event model powers the date view, comparison chart, and explanation. Aegis budget limits remain distinct from cash events; do not silently copy PocketSmith's budget-event semantics. |
| [Float — Cash Flow Management](https://www.floatapp.com/) | Offers monthly, weekly and 13-week views, scenarios, and invoice/bill timing. Public product illustrations and hero were visible; no authenticated forecast was inspected. | A business owner needs to see when receivables arrive relative to obligations. | Short operational cash horizon, base-versus-scenario overlay, receivables/bills drill-down. Accounting integrations and finance-team permissions remain future Aegis work. |
| [Agicap — Cash Flow Forecast](https://agicap.com/en-us/features/cashflow-forecast/) | Separates short/long-term planning, uses accounts payable/receivable inputs, and describes actual-plan variance review. | Forecasts need to be reviewed against what actually happened. | Saved forecast snapshots with timing/amount differences, after the core model is reliable. No imported claims of accuracy or autonomous forecasting. |
| [Xero — Short-term Cash Flow](https://www.xero.com/us/accounting-software/analytics/cash-flow/) | Shows invoice/bill contribution to cash and distinguishes due dates from expected payment dates. | An overdue invoice is a claim on money, not a received balance. | Separate contractual due and expected receipt dates, an outstanding amount, and a source entry behind every projection. Aegis does not issue tax invoices or make payments in this prototype. |
| [YNAB — Assigning Future Income](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/assigning-future-income-an-overview-BJsTo0jCq) | Its official search result describes planning with scheduled transactions and targets while allocating money currently held. The full support page did not expose readable article text to the web tool. | A forecast must not make expected income look like spendable cash today. | Label “Cash today” separately from expected receipts and conditional headroom. This limited inspection does not establish a complete YNAB UI flow. |
