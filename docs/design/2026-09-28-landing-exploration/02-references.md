# Aegis landing — wider reference research

Reviewed 2026-09-28. Ten additional homepages, covering personal finance, adjacent financial services, planning tools, and product presentation. These are design observations and recommendations, not measured conversion results. Earlier Monarch/Copilot/Linear references belong to the first draft and are not counted here.

All ten pages were read through the web tool. Seven rendered hero views were also inspected in a visible Brave window at 1280 × 900. Screenshots are local research evidence, not assets to place in Aegis's public landing page. Some captures contain consent notices, unfinished image loading or animation states. No sign-in or paid service was used.

## Synthesis

1. **Planning is Aegis's most useful differentiator to show.** Sunsama and Daybridge demonstrate why dates and familiar events communicate immediately. Their pattern can transfer without borrowing their identity.
2. **A focused example is stronger than an equal-weight feature inventory.** Chronicle's output selector and Ramp's workflow framing suggest how to keep one scenario continuous across views.
3. **Finance needs both utility and human context.** YNAB and Wealthfront organize parts of their stories around understandable concerns and life situations. Aegis should use its own restrained language and verified functionality.
4. **Ownership belongs in the decision path.** Actual makes setup and control explicit. Aegis can explain its MIT source, self-hosting documentation and export without claiming encryption or offline guarantees it has not demonstrated.
5. **Expression can vary without obscuring the product.** Raycast supports an expressive dark route; planner-first and editorial routes offer substantially different explanations. Choose the narrative before adding visual effects.
